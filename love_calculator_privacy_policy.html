Privacy Policy

Love Calculator – Love Test

Effective date: 9 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Love Calculator – Love Test (“the app”) handles information when you use it. We aim to keep the app simple and privacy-conscious.

1. Information You Enter

You may enter names to generate a fun love-compatibility result. These results are intended for entertainment only and are not scientific or relationship advice.

2. Storage of Names and Results

If the app's history feature is enabled, names and related results may be saved locally on your device so you can view them again. This information is not sent to our own server by the app. You can clear saved history using the available in-app option, if provided. Uninstalling the app may also remove locally stored app data, depending on your device settings.

3. Account and Online Services

The app does not require you to create an account or sign in with a phone number, OTP, or password. The core love-calculator feature is designed to work on your device without an app-operated server.

4. Advertising, Analytics and Third-Party Services

At the time this policy is published, this policy does not claim that the app uses advertising or analytics SDKs. If advertising, analytics, crash-reporting tools, or other third-party SDKs are added, those services may collect information such as device identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics, or approximate location as described in their own policies. This policy and the app's store disclosures should be updated before those services are enabled.

5. Permissions

The app should request only the permissions needed for its features. If a future version requests additional permissions, their purpose will be explained in the app where appropriate and this policy will be updated as necessary.

6. Sharing and Selling of Information

We do not sell names or calculator results entered into the app. The app does not send this information to our own server. Information handled by your device platform or any third-party service, if introduced in a future version, is subject to that service's own practices.

7. Children's Privacy

The app is a general entertainment tool and is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been provided to us directly, please contact us so we can review the concern. Do not enter sensitive personal information into the app.

8. Data Security and Retention

Locally stored history remains on your device until you clear it, the app removes it, or your device removes the app's data. Keep your device secure and avoid entering information you do not want stored on it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features or data practices change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
